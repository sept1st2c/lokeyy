NORTHBOUND GEAR — BRAND VOICE & MESSAGING GUIDE
================================================

1. BRAND PERSONALITY
Northbound Gear is the quietly capable friend who's already done the hike
you're planning. We are calm, direct, and encouraging -- never loud,
never salesy. We respect the customer's intelligence and their time.

Voice traits:
- Grounded, not hyped. We describe what a product does before we praise it.
- Warm but economical. Short sentences. No filler.
- Confident without superlatives. We do not say "the best," "revolutionary,"
  "game-changing," or "industry-leading." Ever.
- Encouraging, not commanding. We invite, we don't instruct.

2. PREFERRED VOCABULARY (use these)
- "built for" (not "designed for")
- "trail-tested" (not "field-proven")
- "packs light" (not "ultralight" as a marketing buzzword on its own --
  always pair with a concrete number, e.g. "packs light at 340g")
- "keeps you moving" (not "performance-driven")
- "made to last" (not "durable" used alone as a claim)

3. FORBIDDEN / BANNED TERMS (never use these in any customer-facing copy)
- "game-changing"
- "revolutionary"
- "industry-leading"
- "best-in-class"
- "unleash" / "unlock your potential"
- "synergy"
- "seamless" (overused, says nothing concrete)
- Any unqualified superlative ("the best," "the only," "nothing compares")
  without a specific, checkable fact backing it up.

4. TABOO TOPICS (do not reference)
- Do not compare Northbound Gear directly to named competitor brands.
- Do not make medical or safety claims (e.g. "prevents injury," "waterproof
  in all conditions") -- use "weather-resistant" and defer to product specs.
- Do not discuss pricing strategy or margins in customer-facing copy.

5. TONE BY CHANNEL
- Email: warm, one clear call-to-action, no more than 2 exclamation points
  per email, sign-off is always "See you out there."
- Social captions: short, present tense, can be playful, still no banned terms.
- Product pages: factual first, sensory second. Lead with a spec or use-case,
  then one sentence of feeling.

6. EXAMPLES

  Don't: "Our revolutionary new jacket is the best-in-class shell on the
  market -- it will unleash your potential on every trail!"

  Do: "The Ridgeline Shell packs light at 310g and keeps you moving
  through wind and drizzle. Built for the long approach, not just the
  summit photo."

  Don't: "This tent is 100% waterproof and will prevent any injury from
  bad weather, guaranteed better than [Competitor Brand]."

  Do: "The Basecamp 2P is weather-resistant to a 3000mm hydrostatic rating
  and trail-tested through two Pacific Northwest winters."

7. CLAIMS THAT MUST ALWAYS CITE A SPEC
Any durability, weight, or weather-resistance claim must be paired with a
specific number or test condition already present in this document or the
product spec sheet. Do not state a bare adjective ("durable," "tough",
"waterproof") as a standalone claim.
