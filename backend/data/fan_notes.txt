SOME NOTES ON BARCO'S VIBE (personal blog draft, not official)

Hey all, just jotting down my own take on how Barco "sounds" as a brand
after reading through a bunch of their stuff and press releases. Take
this with a grain of salt, I'm not a Barco employee, just a display/AV
nerd who follows them closely.

My take: Barco comes across pretty confident and future-facing. They
love talking about "visioneering" (their word, kind of a mashup of
vision + engineering I think?) and they're always doing the big
"what if" style question in their marketing, like "what if surgery
could happen from anywhere." Very inspirational-sounding, almost a
little sci-fi at times.

They also seem to always call themselves just "Barco," never really
using long corporate-speak names for their divisions unless it's some
investor-relations document. I think that's on purpose, like a "one
brand" thing rather than a house-of-brands thing. Could be wrong on
the exact reasoning though, this is just my read as an outsider.

One thing I've noticed: they write pretty plainly, not a lot of
flowery language, short sentences, kind of matter-of-fact. Not super
jargon-heavy either, even in stuff about pretty technical products
like projectors and LED walls, which I actually appreciate.

Anyway that's just my quick impression, happy to be corrected by
someone who actually works there. Not an official style guide obviously,
just a fan's read on the pattern.
